# Candidate Interviewing

## When to activate

Use for discovery or clarification of jobs, technical projects and accomplishments.

## Procedure

1. Start with open-ended project or role questions.
2. Probe candidate-owned decisions, tools, stakeholders and constraints.
3. Ask about outcomes and supporting artifacts.
4. Clarify chronology and team versus individual responsibility.
5. Reflect statements for candidate correction.
6. Record unanswered details as persistent FUP proposals.
7. Invite optional previous careers, personal interests, volunteer work and product/customer familiarity; do not assume consent to publish. For durable context use CTX- records via the candidate-context-interviewing skill.

## Expected output

A structured interview summary with confirmed, tentative and unresolved fields.

## Domain-specific caution

Do not pressure for metrics or suggest answers to fill a gap.

## Standing safeguards

- Follow the invoking agent's AGENTS.md and actual tool/filesystem permissions. This skill does not grant new powers.
- Respect human-in-the-loop approval for all configured gated tools. For authoritative profile writes, obtain explicit candidate approval independently of tool approval.
- Preserve stable EXP-, PROJECT-, ACC-, SKILL-, EDU-, CERT-, EVID- and FUP- identifiers and link statements to the relevant records.
- Do not invent jobs, qualifications, scope, technology usage, quantifiable results, evidence or completion status.
- Open FUP records do not by themselves block producing an otherwise supported resume or cover letter.

## Scope

Intended agent group: **Candidate Profile Development Agent**. For other agents, use only if their AGENTS.md explicitly permits the operation; route disallowed work to the owner.
