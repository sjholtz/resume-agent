# Company Research

## When to activate

Use when preparing a targeted cover letter or identifying employer priorities.

## Procedure

1. List research questions relevant to role.
2. Check official employer sources and dates.
3. Corroborate important current facts where appropriate.
4. Separate fact from inference.
5. Identify technically relevant company context.
6. Save short source-linked findings.
7. Look for genuinely job-relevant company products, customers or publicly documented optional activities that might connect with known CTX-/EXP- records; cite the source.
8. For a missing possible connection, ask a nonleading, optional question and use the employer-candidate-connections skill. Do not infer the candidate shares an interest or is authorized to disclose it.
9. Avoid irrelevant brand talking points.

## Expected output

A concise company brief with source URL, date and application relevance.

## Domain-specific caution

Public company information cannot establish candidate experience.

## Standing safeguards

- Follow the invoking agent's AGENTS.md and actual tool/filesystem permissions. This skill does not grant new powers.
- Respect human-in-the-loop approval for all configured gated tools. For authoritative profile writes, obtain explicit candidate approval independently of tool approval.
- Preserve stable EXP-, PROJECT-, ACC-, SKILL-, EDU-, CERT-, EVID- and FUP- identifiers and link statements to the relevant records.
- Do not invent jobs, qualifications, scope, technology usage, quantifiable results, evidence or completion status.
- Open FUP records do not by themselves block producing an otherwise supported resume or cover letter.

## Scope

Intended agent group: **Application Development Agent**. For other agents, use only if their AGENTS.md explicitly permits the operation; route disallowed work to the owner.
